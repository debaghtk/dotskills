# MISSION.md Format

`MISSION.md` is the short compass for every teaching decision.

```md
# Mission: {Topic}

## Why
{The concrete real-world outcome behind learning this topic.}

## Success looks like
- {A specific, observable capability}
- {Another observable capability}

## Constraints
- {Time, budget, prior commitments, or learning preferences}

## Out of scope
- {Adjacent topics intentionally deferred}
```

Keep one mission per workspace, concrete rather than abstract, and short enough to fit on one screen. Interview the user when the reason is vague. Update the mission when reality changes, with the user's confirmation.
